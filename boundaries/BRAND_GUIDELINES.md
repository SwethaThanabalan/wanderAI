# wanderAI Brand Guidelines

Version: 0.1.0

Status: Alpha

---

# Purpose

This document defines the visual and emotional identity of wanderAI.

The goal is not to create a complete design system, but to ensure a consistent experience throughout the Alpha.

---

# Brand Mission

Help travelers experience journeys with confidence, curiosity, and companionship.

---

# Brand Vision

Become the all-in-one travel companion that supports travelers before, during, and after every journey.

---

# Brand Personality

wanderAI should feel:

- Helpful
- Curious
- Adventurous
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Friendly
- Intelligent
- Encouraging

It should never feel:

- Corporate
- Cold
- Overwhelming
- Complicated
- Distracting
- Gimmicky

---

# Brand Voice

Write as if you're a knowledgeable local friend.

Speak naturally.

Be encouraging.

Be concise.

Avoid sounding robotic.

Examples

✅ "Lake Crescent is one of the clearest lakes in the Pacific Northwest."

✅ "You're only five minutes away from a beautiful viewpoint."

Avoid

❌ "Navigation event successfully completed."

❌ "Destination metadata loaded."

---

# Visual Style

The interface should feel:

- warm
- welcoming
- modern
- outdoors-inspired
- uncluttered

Large photography should be the primary visual element.

---

# Color Palette

## Primary

Forest Green

Represents exploration and nature.

## Secondary

Warm Brown

Inspired by hiking trails and the wanderAI mascot.

## Accent

Lake Blue

Used for maps, water, and interactive elements.

## Background

Warm Cream

Creates a calm reading experience.

## Success

Green

## Warning

Amber

## Error

Red

---

# Typography

Primary Font

SF Pro

Use native iOS typography styles whenever possible.

Hierarchy

Display

Title

Headline

Body

Caption

---

# Spacing

Use an 8-point spacing system.

Primary spacing values:

- 8
- 16
- 24
- 32
- 48

Maintain generous whitespace.

---

# Corner Radius

Cards

20pt

Buttons

16pt

Sheets

28pt

Maps

20pt

---

# Icons

Use SF Symbols.

Prefer outlined icons.

Filled icons indicate selected states.

Avoid mixing icon styles.

---

# Imagery

Photography should emphasize:

- scenic landscapes
- national parks
- hiking
- lakes
- mountains
- forests
- road trips
- authentic travel moments

Avoid stock-photo aesthetics where possible.

---

# The wanderAI Mascot

A curious brown toy poodle explorer.

The mascot represents:

- companionship
- curiosity
- adventure
- warmth

The mascot appears during:

- onboarding
- loading states
- empty states
- achievements
- travel summaries

The mascot should never distract from core content.

---

# Accessibility

Colors must meet WCAG AA contrast requirements.

Do not rely solely on color to communicate status.

Support Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.

---

# Motion

Animations should feel calm and purposeful.

Recommended duration:

150–250ms

Avoid excessive movement.

Motion should reinforce navigation and progress.

---

# Brand Promise

wanderAI helps travelers experience the journey—not just reach the destination.

Every design decision should reinforce this promise.
